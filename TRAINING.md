# Training and match preparation

The player sees opponent characteristics and chooses their own preparation and ring orders.
The default camp is free and follows the player's previous practice, not a prescription based
on opponent type. Camp providers, additional training themes and equipment lists are optional
disclosures. C-grade onboarding stays automatic and balanced; B-grade training direction
remains technical or power.

## What is persistent

- Existing attributes and A-grade style progression keep their original total growth budgets.
- A camp theme shifts 15% of the regular training allocation towards its relevant attribute.
- Theme experience grows once between bouts, by 2–4 points. Relevant equipment and a coach
  improve learning; repeating the preparation screen does not grant more experience.
- Using movement, pressure or counters in a bout also adds bounded experience.
- Equipment purchases unlock practice options and influence later training allocation.
  They no longer grant instant attribute points. Previously saved attributes are retained.

## What belongs to one bout

Preparation records the chosen opponent and the current bout-history count. Its readiness
bonus only applies when both match. Changing the opponent retains learned skills but does
not transfer the opponent-specific bonus. Completed results clear current preparation.
The bout freezes its own training snapshot, including when saved and resumed.

Prepared careers soften the existing tactic triangle by four points and add bounded distance
control (-1.5 to +1.5) and rehearsal effects. Preparation never guarantees success. Body-work
fatigue effects require positive distance control; pacing trades aggression for conservation.
NPC bouts and older bouts without a training snapshot use the original combat model.

## Coaching

The coach selects a training partner and technical or conditioned practice automatically.
Poor condition or an active recovery period selects non-contact technical practice instead.
The corner reports observed movements, stamina and downs, without recommending an order.
Coach level and analysis equipment expand the observed-round sample. Three neutral orders
describe their general intent and cost. The player's current order remains selected until
they change it; a new prepared bout waits for an explicit opening decision. Camp preparation
never overwrites the opening tactic. Quick results also permit choosing an order before entry.
Post-bout review aggregates the player's chosen orders, observed effective-punch advantages
and actual round-by-round energy expenditure. It does not attribute every outcome to one factor.

Opponent AI order names, predicted combat edges and exact matchup adjustments are not shown
by the scouting UI. Internal simulation data remains available for settlement and balance tests.
Existing mid-bout saves can resume their current segment without changing the player's order.

## References and interpretation

- [England Boxing Coaching Handbook Part 1](https://www.englandboxing.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/EB_Boxing-Coaching-Handbook-Part-1_v8-002.pdf):
  purposeful shadow boxing, padwork and technique/conditioned sparring.
- [IBA Coaches Manual](https://www.iba.sport/documents/iba-coach-manual/):
  tactical preparation, different opponent types, distance, conditioning and recovery.

All numbers, unlocks and bonuses are game-design abstractions, not training prescriptions.
